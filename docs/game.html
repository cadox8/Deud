What is Deud?

Deud is a RPG and an adventure game based on 80s RPG games.

Your adventure starts in the quiet village of Springwood, where you life with your family, friends and some ducks. The routine is present on us, except for one day, The Great Day. That day their King came to Springwood with two special people: his daughter and his fiance.
The King, was a very good person, close to his subjects and understanding. His daughter was beautiful, smiling and friendly. On the other hand, his fiance looked like and an authentic idiot.
The day was rewarding, but the worst happened at night. Someone or something came to town, not to trade or make friends, but to kidnap the princess.

Now is your turn. You decided to help the King and rescue the princess from the from the claws of Malilion in order to maintain the prosperity of the kingdom.


What I will find inside?

Playing Deud you will face to terrible monsters, beautiful fairies and a lot of new people in order to help the Princess. Travel through the world of Iastiterra, discover new places and learn one type of magic forgotten a long time ago...
