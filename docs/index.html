About

Deud is a game based on 80s rpg games. Deud is made purely with Java 9 and it should work in most computers (PC, Linux, Mac) and without too much requirements. The idea of the game come when Cadox8, bored, started writing a story. Now, the project have more than one year old and it was re-write too many times. You can send an email to cadox8@gmail.com to contact with the developer. Also, you can find more info about the developer in his Web.
